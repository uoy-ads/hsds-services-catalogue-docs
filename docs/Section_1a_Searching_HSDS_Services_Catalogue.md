## Search Menu

Use the menu on the left to find services that match your specific needs. The search filters can be used individually or in combination:

* __Search Bar__ - Type in the name of the service, organisation, location or technique.
* __Map Interface__ - Navigate the map to search for services in specific geographic locations.
* __Organisation__ - Search by the name of the service provider.
* __Method/Techniques__ - Browse available analytical methods, organised by overall technique type and relevant subcategories.
* __Asset Types__ - Find services suited to specific materials or objects.

Combine multiple filters to narrow your results and identify the most suitable facilities for your research. Click the __x__ next to a search term at the top of the catalogue to remove this filter.
Use the **'Reset Filters'** button at the bottom of search menus to return to the complete service listing.

## Search Results

Your filtered results display as a list of services displaying the following information:

* Organisation name, location and a short description of services.
* A list of Techniques, Asset Types and Tools.

**Interactive Tags** - Click on any underlined Technique or Asset Type to instantly filter all services offering that capability. This creates a new search result focused on the selected term.

**Expanding Lists** - When a list displays '+ x more', click on the link to view the complete range of techniques, asset types, or tools available from that service.

---

Click the **'View Service'** button from any search result to view the full [Service Page](Section_1b_Service_Page.md).
