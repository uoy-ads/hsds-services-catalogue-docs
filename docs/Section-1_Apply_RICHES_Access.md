# Apply for RICHeS Access

Within each service page you have the option to click the 'Apply for RICHeS Access' button. This link will allow you to find out more about a scheme that provides funds to support users and the service provider to access these services.

The [RICHeS Access Fund](https://www.riches.ukri.org/funding/riches-access-fund/) helps researchers, organisations and heritage professionals gain access to cutting-edge equipment, collections and expertise, including mobile units. By covering costs that might otherwise be a barrier, the fund encourages collaborative, interdisciplinary and multidisciplinary research while showcasing the value and impact of a national heritage science infrastructure.

Applications to the scheme can be made via the RICHeS website. For further information either click the 'Apply for RICHeS Access' button within your chosen service or visit the [RICHeS Access Fund pages](https://www.riches.ukri.org/funding/riches-access-fund/).
