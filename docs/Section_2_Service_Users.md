# Introduction

Approved organisations can add services and manage how they appear to users in the public catalogue. You control what the heritage science community can discover about your organisation. This includes your research facilities, available methods, and key contacts.

This guide explains how to:

* [Register via the User Management Application (UMA)](./Section_2a_Register_via_UMA.md)
* [Log in to the Service Provider menu](./Section_2b_Log_into_the_Catalogue.md)
* [Create, edit, and publish service entries](./Section_2c_Create_and_Edit_Entries.md)
* [Manage tools, metadata, and public facing catalogue information](./Section_2d_Manage_Entries.md)
